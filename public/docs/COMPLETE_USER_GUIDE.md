## 1. Quick Start: The 4-Step Production Pipeline

1. **Input Your Idea or Transcript**: Choose **Topic**, paste a **Video URL**, upload a **File**, or dictate using the **Speech-to-Text Mic**.
2. **Tune Your Production Parameters**: Pick your **Domain (Niche)**, **Tone**, **Target Audience**, and **Script Length**.
3. **Generate Polished Voiceover Script**: Click **GENERATE SCRIPT**. Preview with the built-in **Listen AI (TTS)** player.
4. **Export Visuals & Metadata**: Generate timestamped **Scene Prompts**, calculate camera shots with the **Shots Calculator**, optimize YouTube CTR metadata, and synthesize viral thumbnail concepts.

## 2. Left Sidebar: Core Script Configuration Engine

### Voice Persona (Speaker)
- **What It Does**: Switches between **FEMALE** and **MALE** speaker personas for voiceover synthesis and text phrasing.
- **Available Options**: `FEMALE` | `MALE`
- **Why You Need to Adjust This**:
  - **Female Persona**: Optimized for emotional storytelling, healthcare, beauty, self-care, mindfulness, lifestyle vlogs, educational explainers, and welcoming customer tutorials.
  - **Male Persona**: Engineered for deep authoritative documentaries, investigative crime, historical retrospectives, automotive, finance/crypto, sports commentary, and high-energy trailers.

### Domain (50+ SEO Niches)
- **What It Does**: Selects from over 50 specialized industry niches to inject authentic vocabulary, technical terms, and search-optimized keyword clusters.
- **Why You Need to Adjust This**:
  - Selecting **Medical, Health & Biotech** instructs the model to use clinical terminology and balanced disclaimers. Selecting **Finance & Crypto** ensures accurate trading terms. Selecting **Islamic Studies** embeds dignified phrasing, Quranic context, and proper greetings.
  - Elevates organic YouTube search discoverability by embedding high-intent niche keywords throughout the script.

### Tutorial & Literature Tool (50+ Tones)
- **What It Does**: Calibrates the emotional frequency, rhythm, and literary style of the narration (e.g. *Fast Paced Explainer*, *Dramatic Narrative*, *Warm Friendly Conversational*, *Academic Expert*, *Comedy & Sarcastic*, *Poetic Shayari Vibe*, *Luxury Elegance*, *Islamic Tone*).
- **Why You Need to Adjust This**:
  - Viewer drop-off occurs when the emotional tone mismatches the video topic. Matching the tone guarantees your audience stays engaged from start to finish.

### Transformation Option (Multi-Language Engine)
- **What It Does**: Selects the target language and script writing system for the polished output (Urdu Nastaliq, Urdu Roman, English, Hindi with Urdu phrasing, Spanish, Arabic, French, German, and 40+ languages).
- **Why You Need to Adjust This**:
  - **Urdu Roman** is the highest-converting script format for South Asian creators on Shorts, TikTok, and Reels because voiceover artists and teleprompters can read it effortlessly.
  - **Nastaliq Urdu** serves broadcast, Islamic reminders, and traditional publications.
  - **English Polished** unlocks international CPMs in Tier-1 countries.

### Target Audience (8-Tier Demographic Engine)
- **What It Does**: Adapts sentence complexity, vocabulary, pace, cultural honorifics, and relatable analogies to a specific age demographic (All Ages, Kids up to 10, Teens up to 20, Young Adults up to 30, Adults up to 40, Men up to 50, Senior Men up to 60, Elders up to 80).
- **Why You Need to Adjust This**:
  - Guarantees that viewers in your specific age demographic feel directly addressed with age-appropriate vocabulary and pacing.

### Target Regions / Countries
- **What It Does**: Customizes regional slang, localized currencies, geographic metaphors, and cultural touchpoints (Pakistan, India, USA, UK, UAE, Saudi Arabia, etc.).
- **Why You Need to Adjust This**:
  - Injects accurate regional economic references (e.g. PKR vs USD), local proverbs, and culturally grounded idioms.

### Script Length (Word Count vs. Video Duration)
- **What It Does**: Sets your precise script output volume using two intuitive calculation modes:
  - **By Word Count**: Manual slider or number input (0 to 20,000 words) with presets (300, 1500, 10k, 20k).
  - **By Video Duration**: Enter desired target video minutes (e.g., 15 minutes) with automated ~145 WPM conversion.
- **Why You Need to Adjust This**:
  - Keeps 60-second shorts strictly between 120 and 150 words to avoid rushed speech, and ensures 10-minute explainers reach the 1,500-word mark for optimal mid-roll retention.

### Greeting (Prefix)
- **What It Does**: Customizes the opening line of your video script (Asslamoalaikum, Adaab, Namaste, Greetings, Welcome, None, or custom text).
- **Why You Need to Adjust This**:
  - Establishes immediate community rapport and channel brand identity in the first 3 seconds.

### Custom Hook Input & Sectional Structuring
- **What It Does**: Enter a custom hook question (e.g. *"Kya aap jante hain?"*) and toggle the 3-act retention structure (Curiosity Hook 0-5s, Core Body, Call-To-Action).
- **Why You Need to Adjust This**:
  - 70% of viewers drop off in the first 10 seconds if no hook is present. The 3-act structure maximizes viewer retention curves.

### Fast Lite Mode Toggle
- **What It Does**: Strips out visual bracketed cues (`[B-Roll: ...]`) for fast, clean voiceover audio generation.
- **Why You Need to Adjust This**:
  - Toggle ON when you only need clean narration text for voice recording. Toggle OFF when you need camera and visual scene cues.

## 3. Script Workspace: Input Sources & Polished Voiceover

- **Input Source**: Choose between **Topic** (generates draft from scratch), **Video URL** (extracts transcript from YouTube/TikTok/FB/IG), and **Files** (imports `.txt` or `.pdf`).
- **RAW SOURCE SCRIPT Box**: Primary workspace with character count, speech-to-text mic with live Gemini waveform, and clear button.
- **POLISHED V.O. SCRIPT Output Box**: Displays transformed script with 100% Plagiarism-Free badge, in-browser **Listen AI (TTS)** voice simulator, copy, and download actions.
- **Quick Instant Transformation Buttons**: 4 one-click header triggers to instantly re-translate output into Hindi, Urdu Roman, Urdu Nastaliq, or English.

## 4. Cinematic Storyboard & Scene Prompt Creator

- **TRANSCRIPT INPUT**: Configure Category, Scenes count (1 to 100), Format (16:9, 9:16, 1:1, none), and Prompt Line depth (3 to 50 lines; default 15 lines).
- **Duration-Based Shots Calculator**: Enter video minutes and shot seconds to auto-calculate the total required scene cuts.
- **GENERATED SCENE PROMPTS**: Numbered scene cards with individual copy, download, and single-scene regenerate buttons, plus batch COPY ALL, `.TXT`, and `.DOCX` Word document downloads.
