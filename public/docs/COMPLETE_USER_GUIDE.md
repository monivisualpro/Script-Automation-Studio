# Script Automation Studio - Complete User & Prompting Guide

Welcome to **Script Automation Studio**! This document serves as your complete user manual, prompt engineering guide, and controls reference dictionary.

## 📌 1. Quick Start Workflow

1. **Input Your Content**: Paste your rough idea, notes, or article into the **Raw Script Input** box.
2. **Choose Domain & Tone**: Select your domain (e.g. *Artificial Intelligence*, *Finance*, *Islamic*, *Food*) and preferred Tone (e.g. *Warm Friendly Conversational*, *Fast Paced Explainer*).
3. **Select Script Transformation**: Choose *English Polisher*, *Urdu*, *Roman Urdu/Hindi*, *Shorts/Reels*, or *Documentary*.
4. **Set Word Count**: Adjust word count or video duration target (e.g. 300 words ~ 2 minutes).
5. **Generate**: Click **Generate Polished Script**.
6. **Split into B-Roll Shots**: Click **Shots Calculator** to generate timestamped B-roll camera instructions.
