1. THE ANATOMY OF A HIGH-RETENTION SCRIPT PROMPT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every viral video script consists of 4 foundational building blocks:
1. THE HOOK (0:00 - 0:05): Pattern interrupt that stops scrolling.
2. THE PROMISE (0:05 - 0:15): Tells the viewer why watching until the end matters.
3. THE VALUE DELIVERABLE (Body): Structured, concise information with visual cues.
4. THE CALL TO ACTION (CTA): Simple, natural push to subscribe or comment.
