Platform Overview & Multi-Provider Architecture

Engineered for video creators, educators, editors, and digital marketers

Script Automation Studio transforms raw ideas, fragmented thoughts, voice transcripts, or YouTube URLs into polished, high-retention video scripts. It supports Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, and Local LLMs (LM Studio / Ollama / Bionic LLM Studio), alongside scene B-roll direction, high-CTR YouTube metadata, and thumbnail prompt generation.

Retention First

Strict opening hook triggers, pattern interrupts, and concise narrative pacing tailored to your chosen age bracket.

Production Ready

Generates scene-by-scene camera prompts, duration-based shot calculations, and high-CTR YouTube metadata suites.

02

Core Controls & Input Box Dictionary

Detailed breakdown of every input, button, and parameter

BOX Raw Source Script / Topic / Video URL

Top Workspace

Enter a topic prompt, extract a YouTube video transcript via URL, upload a `.txt` / `.pdf` file, or dictate directly with Voice Input. Rough bullet points or stream-of-consciousness drafts work great.

KEY Personal AI Provider & Key Status

Top Navigation Header

Choose your preferred AI backend (Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Built-In Local Engine, or LM Studio / Ollama). Keys are stored locally on your machine in `.env` or local desktop settings.

SIDEBAR Domain, Target Audience & Regions

Left Sidebar

Grounds the script in 50+ SEO niches, adapts vocabulary across 8 age brackets (All Ages through Elders 80), and localizes cultural idioms and currencies for your chosen target countries.

TOGGLE Hooks, Body & Conclusion + Fast Lite Mood

Generation Console

Enforces the 3-part retention structure (0–5s Hook, Core Body, Call-To-Action) and lets you toggle Fast Lite Mood for streamlined voiceover-only output.

03

Transformation Modes & Languages

Instant multi-language rephrasing and cultural voiceover adaptation

Single-Language Output

Generates your polished script strictly in the single language selected via Transformation Option (including Hindi (Urdu Wording/Accent), Urdu Roman, Urdu Nastaliq (RTL), and English), keeping generation fast and token-efficient.

40+ Global Languages

Use the searchable Transformation Option dropdown in the sidebar for Arabic, Farsi, Turkish, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and regional languages.

Prompting & Command Masterclass

How to write inputs that generate 10/10 viral outputs

Template A: Tech Shorts / Reels

"Topic: Why Quantum Computers will make regular encryption obsolete in 2026. Tone: Fast Paced Explainer Target: 140 words, high curiosity hook."

Expected Output: High-energy opening, simple qubit metaphor, visual cues, subscribe CTA.

Template B: Mystery / Documentary

"Topic: The mystery of flight MH370 radar anomaly. Tone: Dramatic Narrative Voice Persona: Male / Deep Documentary Length: 800 words."

Expected Output: Atmospheric pacing, suspense pauses, radar timestamps, cinematic storyboard readiness.
