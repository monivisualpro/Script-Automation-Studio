1. Header & Top Navigation Controls

Brand Switcher (🔴 Red / 🔵 Blue) Header

Dynamically alters primary and secondary accent colors (#FF073A / #2774FC) across all buttons, borders, highlights, and icons.

Day / Night Theme Toggle Header

Switches between high-contrast dark room mode (#0D0E12) and bright daylight studio mode (#FAFAFA) to protect eye health.

Image Studio Modal (Wand Icon) Header

Opens the AI visual suite with aspect ratio options (16:9 YouTube, 9:16 Shorts, 1:1 Square) to generate thumbnails or B-roll shots.

Settings & Model Selection Header

Adjusts Gemini engine parameters (Gemini 2.5 Flash, Gemini Pro), generation temperature, and safety filtering thresholds.

2. Scriptmaking & Configuration Controls

Voice Persona Dropdown Sidebar

Specifies voiceover delivery style: Energetic Hype Creator, Deep Documentary Narrator, Warm Storyteller, or Corporate Professional.

Shots Calculator (Scene Splitter) Post-Tool

Calculates speech timing and divides output scripts into timestamped video scenes with B-roll visual and audio sound effect cues.

Check Plagiarism & Originality Post-Tool

Performs an originality calculation on the script body to confirm zero-risk copyright safety and avoid demonetization.

Transformation Option (Language) Thumbnail & Script

Controls target linguistic script: Urdu Nastaliq, Roman Urdu, Hindi, Arabic, English, Spanish, French, Japanese, Russian, etc.
